Refreshing the original Metagigs brand website experience

Overview

Metagigs is a game coaching marketplace designed to help studios boost player retention through customized training and progression systems.

By redesigning its official brand website, I transformed complex mechanisms into a clear and engaging narrative that highlights both the product’s value and long-term vision.

My Role
UXUI Designer
Team
UXUI Designer*1, Front-end Engineer*1, Project Manager*1
Duration
1 month
Tools
Figma

Goal

Enhance the Metagigs brand website by improving its information architecture, visual hierarchy, and overall user experience, ensuring that the platform’s mechanisms and value are communicated more effectively to investors and potential stakeholders.

Let's see the final redesign website first

Pain Points

Why Redesign Was Needed

The previous Metagigs website was cluttered and unclear, making it hard for investors to understand the product’s value.

The following sections highlight key issues that required a redesign.

Header & Hero section
  1. Navigation was limited and redundant, with missing key tabs and duplicate links.
  2. The hero headline was vague and failed to communicate what Metagigs offered.
  3. No clear value proposition left visitors uncertain about the product’s purpose.
Value Proposition Section
  1. Focused on roles (“Coach, Guide, Advisor”) instead of clearly stating Metagigs’ unique benefits.
  2. 3D characters distracted attention from the core message.
  3. Talked to players, but didn’t explain the value for game developers.
Monetization Section
  1. Visuals are cluttered and distract from the main idea.
  2. The revenue model is explained vaguely, without clear steps.
  3. Claims like “increase retention” feel generic and unsupported.

Defining Priorities

From Challenges to a Clear Design Direction

In our initial workshop with the client, we aligned on two priorities for the redesign:

Clearly communicating Metagigs’ core value
Explaining how the system works in a simple and engaging way

Having uncovered the main pain points, we moved on to align with the client and structure the redesign around clarity and impact ✩

↓

Design Solution

Shaping the New Metagigs Website

The redesigned interface prioritizes clear information hierarchy and intuitive navigation, enabling investors to quickly grasp Metagigs' value proposition and core mechanisms.

01 Redefining the First Impression

  • Introduced smooth animations to create a more dynamic and engaging entry
  • Added a clear “Explore More” CTA to guide users deeper into the site
  • Organized essential navigation tabs for easier access to key content

02 Highlighting Core Value Drivers

  • Clearly framed the platform’s unique value for retention, monetization, and community
  • Organized benefits into three concise pillars for faster understanding
  • Strengthened visuals and copy to better align with investor expectations

03 Clarifying the Journey

  • Simplified the flow into three clear steps
  • Enhanced readability with visual hierarchy
  • Aligned tone for a consistent brand voice
